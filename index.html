Set up your camera, then throw

  1. Full body in frame, head to feet, with a little room for your reach.
  2. Camera at chest height, 2.5 to 3.5 m away, turned about 45° off your front. A straight side view hides your back arm.
  3. 60 fps or faster. For the most accurate numbers, record slow-mo on your phone and load it as a video.
  4. Good light, plain background. Fast hands blur in dim rooms and the tracker loses them.

Diagnostics: start the camera or a video.

Height sets the scale. The first second of tracking measures your arm length, so start with your full body visible.
